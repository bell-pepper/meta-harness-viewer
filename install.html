Harness HUD on your glasses

Add a launcher entry for your Meta Ray-Ban Display glasses.

Add with Meta AI

On your phone, tap the button to open Meta AI. Follow its prompts and confirm on the glasses if requested. If viewing this on a computer, scan this QR code with your phone.

Manual alternative

  1. Open Meta AI and select your Display glasses.
  2. Look for App connections → Web apps → Add a web app in the device settings. Labels may differ by software version.
  3. Name: Harness HUD
    URL: https://bell-pepper.github.io/meta-harness-viewer/
  4. Complete setup, then look for Harness HUD in the glasses’ app grid.

If Web apps is unavailable, check Meta AI and glasses updates and Meta’s developer setup instructions. Developer access may be required. We have not yet verified installation on your device.

First glasses check

  1. Launch Harness HUD from its glasses icon.
  2. Select Documents → Harness test datasheet.
  3. The document opens full screen. Press Enter / Select to show page and zoom controls. Controls hide when you zoom; arrows then move the page. Press Enter / Select to show controls again.
  4. At 3× zoom, move to the four labeled page corners and check readability. Back from reading returns to the document list.

The included data is illustrative. The viewer uses prepared page images from the included two-page PDF. Private Google Drive access and arbitrary PDF loading are not part of this milestone.
